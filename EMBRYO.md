# Embryo Router Daemon

Embryo Router Daemon is Embryo-Zero's fork of [frr](https://github.com/FRRouting/frr) (adf6b91, commit adf6b91). It does BGP/OSPF routing for our own network (ASN later).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYING) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
